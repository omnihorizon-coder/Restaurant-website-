Our Story

Built around fire, flavor, and people.

Ember & Spoon began with a simple belief: extraordinary food should feel personal, memorable, and worth gathering around.

Where It Started

We wanted a restaurant that felt like home.

Ember & Spoon was born from a love of open flame cooking, seasonal ingredients, and the conversations that happen around a great table.

We bring together familiar flavors, thoughtful technique, and a modern perspective to create dishes that are expressive without losing their sense of comfort.

For us, hospitality is just as important as what's on the plate. Every visit should feel warm, considered, and worth remembering.

Ember & Spoon Crafted with fire. Served with passion.
Our Philosophy

Simple principles. Serious craft.

We don't believe great food needs unnecessary complexity. It needs good ingredients, patient preparation, and people who care about the result.

01

Respect the Ingredient

We let quality ingredients lead the dish instead of hiding them behind unnecessary decoration.

02

Cook with Intention

Flame, heat, texture, and timing all have a purpose. Precision gives simple food extraordinary character.

03

Make People Feel Welcome

Great hospitality means paying attention, creating comfort, and making every guest feel genuinely looked after.
